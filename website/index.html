在 Rhino 里，把模型变成效果图Turn your Rhino model into a rendering

Chameleon 是 Rhino 的 AI 渲染插件。截取当前视口，写一句想要的效果，几十秒后得到写实的渲染图；不满意就接着改，每一版都留在历史里。

Chameleon is an AI rendering plug-in for Rhino. Capture the viewport, describe the look you want, and get a photorealistic rendering in under a minute. Not quite right? Keep refining; every version stays in the history.

开始使用Get started 注册账号Create an account

能做什么What it does

视口直接出图Render from the viewport

保留模型的构图和视角，生成写实渲染。支持 1K、2K、4K 和多种画幅比例。

Keeps your composition and camera. 1K, 2K or 4K output in many aspect ratios.

参考图控制风格Guide the style

加入风格、材质、物体参考图，让结果贴近你想要的气氛和质感。

Add style, material and object references to steer the mood and materials.

历史与对比History and comparison

每次生成都记录在版本树里，滑块对比前后效果，随时回到任意一版继续修改。

Every generation is kept in a version tree. Compare before and after with a slider and continue from any version.

批量渲染Batch rendering

一次处理多个命名视图，适合方案汇报时快速出一整套图。

Render several named views at once, ideal for a full set of images for a presentation.

三步开始Three steps

  1. 在本站注册账号，并在钱包里充值或兑换额度。Create an account and top up or redeem credit in the wallet.
  2. 安装插件，在 Rhino 中运行 Chameleon 命令，用账号密码登录。Install the plug-in, run the Chameleon command in Rhino and sign in.
  3. 捕获视口、写下想要的效果，点击「生成图片」。Capture the viewport, describe the look and click "Generate image".
