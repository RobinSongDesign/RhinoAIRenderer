关于 Chameleon

Chameleon（变色龙）是一款 Rhino AI 渲染插件。它把 Rhino 视口里的模型交给图像生成模型，几十秒内得到写实的效果图，并支持参考图、局部修改、版本对比和批量渲染。

我们做什么

  • 视口直接出图：保留构图和视角，支持 1K、2K、4K 与多种画幅比例。
  • 参考图控制风格：用风格、材质、物体参考图引导结果。
  • 历史与对比：每一版都记录在版本树里，可随时回到任意一版继续修改。
  • 按张计费：无需订阅，用多少扣多少。

如何开始

  1. 在 api.rhchameleon.top 注册账号，并充值或兑换额度。
  2. 安装插件，在 Rhino 中运行 Chameleon 命令，用账号密码登录。
  3. 捕获视口，写下想要的效果，生成图片。

About Chameleon

Chameleon is an AI rendering plug-in for Rhino. It sends the model in your Rhino viewport to an image generation model and returns a photorealistic rendering within seconds, with reference images, targeted edits, version comparison and batch rendering.

What it does

  • Render straight from the viewport: keeps your composition and camera, at 1K, 2K or 4K and many aspect ratios.
  • Guide the style with references: style, material and object reference images steer the result.
  • History and comparison: every version is kept in a version tree; go back to any of them and continue.
  • Pay per image: no subscription, you only pay for what you generate.

Getting started

  1. Create an account at api.rhchameleon.top and top up or redeem credit.
  2. Install the plug-in, run the Chameleon command in Rhino and sign in with your account.
  3. Capture the viewport, describe the look you want and generate.
