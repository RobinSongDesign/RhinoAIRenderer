Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

This policy explains how the operator of Chameleon, Robin ("we", "us"), collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use the Chameleon plug-in for Rhino, the website rhchameleon.top and the account service api.rhchameleon.top (together, the "Service").

1. Information we collect

InformationPurposeWhen
User name, e-mail, password (stored hashed; we cannot see it)Registration, sign-in, password recoveryWhen you register or sign in
Tokens (one per computer, named after the computer)Let the plug-in use the Service on your behalfWhen you sign in from the plug-in
Balance, top-up and redemption recordsBilling, reconciliation, refundsWhen you top up or redeem
Usage records: time, model, image size, cost, token name, IP addressBilling, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, legal retentionEach generation or balance query
Cookie holding the website sign-inKeep you signed in on the websiteWhen you use the website

Prompts and images: the source images, reference images and prompts you submit, and the images generated, are used only to complete that generation. They pass through our server to the model providers; our server does not store them and does not use them to train models.

The plug-in keeps your generation history and images on your own computer and its token in the system keychain (Windows Data Protection, macOS Keychain). These are never uploaded to us; uninstalling the plug-in or deleting its data folder removes them.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service;
  • To bill, reconcile and process top-ups, redemptions and refunds;
  • To keep accounts and the Service secure and to detect abnormal requests and prohibited content;
  • To meet legal obligations and lawful requests from competent authorities.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for marketing unrelated to the Service.

3. Processors and third parties

To generate images, your prompts and images are transmitted to the following third parties, which process them under their own privacy policies:

RecipientDataPurpose
Image generation API provider (APIMart)Prompts, source and reference images, generated imagesCalling the models; generated images are kept on its servers temporarily and expire after 24 to 72 hours
Model provider (Google Gemini)Same as aboveGenerating images
Cloud server provider (Tencent Cloud)All data on our serverHosting the Service

Apart from the above and where required by law, we do not provide your personal information to other third parties without your consent.

4. Storage location and cross-border transfer

Our server is located in Tokyo, Japan, and the image generation services above are also outside mainland China. Using the Service means that your account information, usage records and the prompts and images you submit for generation are transferred outside mainland China for processing. We protect them with the measures in Section 6. If you do not agree to this transfer, please do not use the Service.

5. Retention

  • Account information: until you close your account.
  • Usage and top-up records: at least six months as required by law; deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.
  • Prompts and images: not stored on our server.

6. How we protect information

  • All traffic between the website, the plug-in and our server is encrypted with HTTPS;
  • Passwords are stored with one-way hashing; the plug-in never stores your password;
  • Server logs do not record prompts, images or token values;
  • Only necessary personnel can access the server and the administration console.

In the event of a personal information security incident, we will inform you and report to the authorities as required by law.

7. Your rights

  • View and update your account details, usage records and balance on the website;
  • Delete any token under "Token management" on the website, which immediately disables the plug-in on that computer;
  • Contact us to obtain a copy of your personal information, correct it, or close your account and have your personal information deleted.

We handle requests within fifteen working days.

8. Minors

The Service is intended for adults. Users under 18 should use it only with the consent and guidance of a guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information of children under 14 and will delete it as soon as we become aware of it.

9. Updates

Updates to this policy are published on the website; significant changes to the purposes, means or scope of processing will be pointed out by a website notice. If the English and Chinese versions of this policy differ, the Chinese version prevails.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your personal information: songqizhen006@gmail.com
